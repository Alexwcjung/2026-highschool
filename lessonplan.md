# 🎵 50-Minute Lesson Plan: Pop Song Listening and Speaking Practice

## 👩‍🎓 1. Learner Profile

The learners are Korean vocational high school students. Although they are high school students, their English proficiency is around the elementary to early middle school level. Many students have low confidence in English, especially in listening and speaking. Therefore, this lesson provides short, familiar, and highly supported English activities.

---

## 🏫 2. Class Setting

- **⏰ Class length:** 50 minutes  
- **🏫 Class type:** Vocational high school English class  
- **🌱 Student level:** Low-proficiency EFL learners  
- **📱 Main tool:** Customized Streamlit English learning app  
- **👨‍🏫 Lesson format:** Teacher-guided, app-supported learning  

The app is used to support repeated listening, speaking practice, pronunciation checking, and self-paced learning.

---

## 🎯 3. Lesson Topic

**Pop Song Listening and Speech Recognition-Based Vocabulary Speaking Practice**

Students first learn English through a pop song. Then, they practice school-life vocabulary in the **Daily English 400** section and check their pronunciation using the speaking quiz.

---

## ✅ 4. Learning Objectives

By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:

1. 🎧 Understand the main idea or mood of a pop song with support from lyrics and captions.
2. 💬 Learn several key expressions from the song.
3. 📚 Listen to school-life vocabulary and identify words they know or do not know well.
4. 🎤 Pronounce selected words and check whether the app recognizes their speech.
5. 🔁 Reflect on which words they need to practice more.

---

## 🗣️ 5. Target Language

## 🧭 6. Teaching Procedure

| Stage | Time | Procedure | Teacher Role | App Integration |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 🌍 Warm-up: World Map Quiz | 5 min | Students answer a few simple map quiz questions. | Leads the warm-up and encourages participation. | World map quiz section |
| 🎵 Pop Song Choice and Background | 5 min | Students choose a song and learn simple background information. | Introduces the song and explains the topic briefly. | Pop song background tab |
| 🎧 Listening with Lyrics | 8 min | Students listen to the song while reading lyrics or captions. | Helps students understand the general meaning. | Lyrics, captions, audio/video |
| ❓ Comprehension and Key Expressions | 7 min | Students answer simple quiz questions and study key expressions. | Checks answers and models pronunciation. | Quiz and key expression tabs |
| 🧩 Sentence Matching | 5 min | Students match English expressions with Korean meanings. | Gives feedback and connects expressions to real use. | Matching game |
| 📚 Daily English 400 Listening Check | 7 min | Students listen to school-life words and mark unknown or difficult words. | Guides students to check their own vocabulary knowledge. | Daily English 400 |
| 🎤 Speaking Quiz with Speech Recognition | 10 min | Students pronounce selected words and check the app’s recognition result. | Monitors students, gives help, and models difficult words. | Speaking Quiz 400 |
| 🔁 Self-Check and Wrap-up | 3 min | Students choose words that were easy and words that need more practice. | Gives final feedback and summarizes the lesson. | Self-check or review function |

---

## 🙋 7. Student Activities

Students will:

- 🌍 answer map quiz questions;
- 🎵 choose and listen to a pop song;
- 👀 read lyrics or captions while listening;
- ❓ answer comprehension questions;
- 💬 learn and match key expressions;
- 📚 listen to school-life vocabulary;
- ✅ check unknown or difficult words;
- 🎤 pronounce words in the speaking quiz;
- 🔁 retry words that are not recognized;
- 📝 complete a short self-check.

---

## 👨‍🏫 8. Teacher Role

The teacher guides the lesson and makes the app activities meaningful. The teacher introduces the topic, explains the song background, models key expressions, monitors students during app practice, and gives feedback on difficult words or pronunciation problems.

The app provides repeated practice and immediate feedback, but the teacher controls the lesson flow and connects the activities to communication.

---

## 📱 9. App Integration

The customized app supports the lesson through:

- 🌍 world map quiz for warm-up;
- 🎵 pop song listening with lyrics and captions;
- ❓ comprehension quiz;
- 💬 key expression learning;
- 🧩 sentence matching game;
- 📚 Daily English 400 vocabulary listening;
- 🎤 speech recognition-based speaking quiz;
- 🔁 self-check or review activities.

The app allows students to learn at their own pace and practice difficult words repeatedly.

---

## 🧾 10. Feedback and Assessment

Assessment is formative. Students are assessed through participation, quiz answers, vocabulary self-check, speaking quiz attempts, and willingness to retry pronunciation.

Feedback is provided through:

- ✅ automatic quiz results;
- 🧩 matching activity feedback;
- 🎤 speech recognition results;
- 👨‍🏫 teacher feedback on common pronunciation problems;
- 🔁 final self-check.

The focus is not perfect pronunciation, but active listening, speaking, checking, and retrying.

---

## 🌱 11. Expected Learning Outcomes

By the end of the lesson, students are expected to:

- 🎧 feel more comfortable listening to English;
- 💬 understand some expressions from a pop song;
- 📚 recognize several school-life words by sound;
- 🎤 pronounce selected words more confidently;
- 🔁 notice which words they need to practice more;
- ✨ experience English learning as interactive and personalized.

---

## 🚀 12. How the App Supports Adaptive Learning

The app supports adaptive learning because students can focus on different words depending on their needs. Students who know many words can move quickly to the speaking quiz, while students who are unsure can listen again and review difficult words.

The speech recognition-based speaking quiz gives each student individual feedback. Students can retry words at their own pace without waiting for the teacher. The teacher then uses students’ difficulties to give targeted feedback. In this way, the app supports self-paced, individualized, and teacher-guided learning.
